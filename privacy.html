Privacy Policy

Welcome to Etoile tredas. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile applications, including GateZentry and Lifeline Alert, and our related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best experience and ensure the core functionality of our apps, we may request access to certain information and device features, including:

  • Personal Data: Name, phone number, and email address provided during registration or visitor entry.
  • Device Permissions: Location (for safety alerts and precise tracking), Camera/Storage (for scanning digital passes and uploading documents), and SMS/Call permissions (for emergency notifications).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to operate and maintain our services. Specifically, we use it to authenticate users, generate digital gate passes, send real-time safety alerts, process payments, and improve overall app security. We do not sell your personal data to any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications integrate with trusted third-party services to function properly. These third parties have their own Privacy Policies addressing how they handle data:

  • Google Services: Google Play Services, Firebase (Cloud Messaging), and Google AdMob (for serving relevant advertisements).
  • Payment Gateways: PayU and Paytm (strictly for securely processing transactions; we do not store your credit card or bank details).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time. To request data deletion, please contact our support team via email with your registered details.
