Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Etoile tredas, we strive to ensure our customers are completely satisfied with our digital products, including GateZentry and Lifeline Alert. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully before making any purchases.

1. Digital Products & In-App Purchases

Due to the nature of digital goods and software, all purchases made directly within our applications are generally non-refundable once the service or premium feature has been accessed or downloaded.

2. Google Play Store Refunds

Since our applications are distributed via the Google Play Store, all payments and subscriptions are processed by Google. If you are not satisfied with a purchase:

  • You may request a refund directly through the Google Play Store within 48 hours of your purchase, subject to Google Play's refund policies.
  • To request a refund from Google, go to your Google Play account, select "Order History," find the transaction, and select "Request a refund."

3. Subscription Cancellations

If you have subscribed to a premium plan on any of our apps, you can cancel your subscription at any time to avoid future charges. Cancellations must be managed through your Google Play Subscriptions settings. Your premium access will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle.

4. Exceptional Circumstances

In cases involving duplicate charges, technical errors preventing access to the app, or unauthorized transactions, please contact our support team. We will investigate the issue and process refunds on a case-by-case basis where applicable.
